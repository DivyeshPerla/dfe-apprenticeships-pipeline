Revisions between published versions

The publisher restates history. Because every version is retained in the bronze layer and the fact table is type‑2, the warehouse can answer what changed, when, and by how much — a question a current‑state‑only model cannot.

Figures revised of which disclosure status changed

Comparison is on typed values, not raw strings. Between 1.0.2 and 2.0 the publisher reformatted roughly 4,200 percentages without changing their meaning; a string‑level diff would have recorded every one as a revision.
